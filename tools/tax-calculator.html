How this income tax calculator works

Income tax is not a single rate applied to your whole salary — both the UK and US use marginal (progressive) bands, so each slice of your income is taxed at a different rate. This calculator applies the official published thresholds, then layers on the payroll taxes most people forget about: National Insurance in the UK and FICA (Social Security + Medicare) in the US.

🇬🇧 United Kingdom — 2026/27 tax year

  • Personal allowance: £12,570 tax-free (frozen since 2021/22), reduced by £1 for every £2 earned above £100,000.
  • England, Wales & NI bands: 20% basic rate to £50,270, 40% higher rate to £125,140, 45% additional rate above.
  • Scotland: six bands from 19% (starter) to 48% (top rate) — the calculator switches automatically when you tick the Scottish box.
  • National Insurance: 8% on earnings between £12,570 and £50,270, then 2% above.
  • Student loans: Plan 1 (£26,900), Plan 2 (£29,385), Plan 4 (£33,795), Plan 5 (£25,000) and Postgraduate (£21,000) thresholds for 2026/27, at 9% (6% for postgraduate) of income above the threshold.

🇺🇸 United States — 2025 & 2026 tax years

  • Seven federal brackets from 10% to 37%, with IRS inflation-adjusted thresholds for both years built in.
  • Standard deduction: $15,750 single / $31,500 joint for 2025; $16,100 / $32,200 for 2026.
  • FICA: 6.2% Social Security (up to the $176,100 wage base) + 1.45% Medicare, plus 0.9% additional Medicare above $200,000.
  • State tax: enter your state's typical rate — nine states charge no income tax at all.

Why your take-home pay matters more than your salary

Two people on the same headline salary can take home very different amounts depending on pension contributions, student loan plan, filing status and state. Use the pension / 401(k) field to see how pre-tax retirement saving lowers your tax bill — in many cases every £100 or $100 contributed only costs you £58–£72 (or $63–$78) of take-home pay.

FAQ

Common questions

Is this tax calculator free to use?

Yes — completely free, with no sign-up. Every calculation runs locally in your browser and nothing you type is stored or sent to a server.

Which tax year does the calculator use?

The UK version uses the 2026/27 tax year (6 April 2026 – 5 April 2027). The US version lets you choose between the 2025 tax year (returns filed in 2026) and the 2026 tax year, using official IRS inflation-adjusted figures.

Does the UK calculator handle Scottish income tax?

Yes. Tick the “I pay Scottish income tax” box and the calculator switches to Scotland's six bands — starter 19%, basic 20%, intermediate 21%, higher 42%, advanced 45% and top 48%.

Does the US calculation include state income tax?

Federal tax and FICA are calculated precisely. State tax is estimated from the flat rate you enter, because each state has its own brackets and deductions — it's a close approximation for planning, not a filing figure.

Why is my marginal rate different from my effective rate?

Your marginal rate is the tax charged on your next pound or dollar of income; your effective rate is total tax divided by total income. Because lower slices of income are taxed at lower rates, your effective rate is always below your marginal rate.
