How capital gains tax works in 2026

Capital gains tax (CGT) is charged on the profit you make when you sell or dispose of an asset — not on the sale price. Your gain is the sale proceeds minus the original cost and allowable expenses (broker fees, legal costs, improvement costs for property).

🇬🇧 UK rules (2026/27)

ItemAmount / rate
Annual exempt amount£3,000 per person
Gains within the basic-rate band18%
Gains above the basic-rate band24%
Assets inside an ISA or pension0% — always CGT-free

Since 30 October 2024 the 18%/24% rates apply to all chargeable assets, so shares and crypto are now taxed at the same rates as residential property. Gains are added on top of your income to decide which rate applies. UK residential property disposals must be reported and paid within 60 days of completion.

🇺🇸 US rules (2026 tax year)

  • Short-term gains (assets held one year or less) are taxed as ordinary income — up to 37% federally.
  • Long-term gains use the 0% / 15% / 20% brackets. For 2026, single filers pay 0% up to $49,450 of taxable income, 15% up to $545,500, and 20% above that.
  • High earners (MAGI over $200k single / $250k joint) also pay the 3.8% Net Investment Income Tax.
  • Most states tax capital gains as regular income on top of the federal bill.

Legitimate ways to reduce your CGT bill

  • Use tax wrappers: UK ISAs and pensions, US 401(k)s and IRAs are CGT-free environments.
  • Harvest the exemption: UK investors can realise up to £3,000 of gains each April–April year tax-free.
  • Hold for the long term (US): crossing the one-year mark can cut your rate from 37% to 15% or less.
  • Offset losses: both countries let you set realised losses against gains in the same year.
  • Spousal transfers (UK): transfers between spouses are tax-free, doubling the household exemption.
FAQ

Common questions

What is the UK capital gains tax allowance for 2026/27?

The annual exempt amount is £3,000 per person. Gains up to this figure each tax year are completely tax-free; only the portion above it is taxed at 18% or 24%.

What are the US long-term capital gains brackets for 2026?

For single filers: 0% up to $49,450 of taxable income, 15% up to $545,500, and 20% above. Married filing jointly: 0% to $98,900 and 15% to $613,700. Short-term gains are simply taxed as ordinary income.

Do I pay capital gains tax when I sell my own home?

Usually no. The UK gives Private Residence Relief on your main home, and the US excludes up to $250,000 of gain ($500,000 for couples) on a primary residence you've lived in for 2 of the last 5 years. Second homes and rentals are taxable.

Is cryptocurrency subject to capital gains tax?

Yes, in both countries. HMRC and the IRS both treat crypto as a chargeable asset, so selling, swapping one coin for another, or spending crypto are all taxable disposals.

Can I deduct losses from my gains?

Yes. Realised losses offset realised gains in the same year, and unused losses can generally be carried forward. In the US you can also deduct up to $3,000 of net losses against ordinary income each year.
