How to compare loans the way regulators intend

The headline interest rate is only part of a loan's cost. Arrangement fees, broker fees and the term length all change what you actually pay. That's why the UK quotes APRC and the US quotes APR — standardised figures that fold fees into the rate so offers can be compared fairly. This calculator does the same job with real numbers: total cost of credit over the full term.

What the numbers mean

  • Monthly payment — fixed instalment from standard amortisation (interest accrues monthly on the falling balance).
  • Total interest — everything you pay above the amount borrowed, excluding fees.
  • Total cost of credit — interest plus fees: the single figure that decides which offer is cheaper.

Classic traps this comparison catches

  • Low rate, high fee: a 5.9% loan with a £295 fee can cost more than a 6.9% loan with none on smaller amounts or shorter terms.
  • Longer term "affordability": stretching from 36 to 60 months cuts the monthly payment but can add hundreds in interest.
  • Card minimum payments: paying the minimum on a card at 24.9% can take decades — model the card as a loan over the months you'd realistically take to clear it.
  • Representative APR: UK lenders only have to give the advertised rate to 51% of accepted applicants — your personal rate may differ, so compare the rates you're actually quoted.

Quick wins before you borrow

  • Check eligibility/soft-search tools first so applications don't dent your credit score.
  • For existing card debt, a 0% balance-transfer card (typical fee 3–4%) usually beats any personal loan if you can clear it within the promotional window.
  • Overpayment flexibility matters: a slightly dearer loan with free overpayments can end up cheaper in real life.
FAQ

Common questions

What's the difference between APR and APRC?

They're the same idea in different markets: a standardised annual rate that includes compulsory fees as well as interest. The US uses APR for consumer credit; the UK uses APR for loans and cards and APRC for mortgages.

Why can a lower interest rate cost more overall?

Because of fees and term length. An upfront fee is paid regardless of rate, so on smaller or shorter loans it can outweigh the interest saving. Always compare total cost of credit, which this tool calculates.

Can I use this for credit card debt?

Yes — enter the balance as the amount, the card's APR, and the number of months you plan to take to clear it. That shows the true cost of carrying the balance versus a loan or 0% transfer.

Does checking loan rates hurt my credit score?

Soft-search eligibility checks don't affect your score. Full applications leave a hard search, so use comparison and eligibility tools first, then apply only for the offer you want.

Is the representative APR the rate I'll get?

Not necessarily. In the UK only 51% of accepted applicants must receive the advertised rate; US rates also vary with credit score. Compare using the personalised quotes you receive.
