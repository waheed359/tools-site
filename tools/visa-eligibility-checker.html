How the two systems actually sort applicants

🇬🇧 The UK runs a points-based system where most work routes require a job offer from a licensed sponsor and a salary above a published floor (£41,700 general threshold for Skilled Worker, with discounts for shortage roles, new entrants and PhDs). 🇺🇸 The US runs a category system where the binding constraints are employer sponsorship, annual caps and — for green cards — per-country queues.

🇬🇧 The main UK routes in one line each

  • Skilled Worker: sponsored job at graduate level, salary ≥ £41,700 or the occupation's going rate — the workhorse route to settlement in 5 years.
  • Graduate: 2 years (3 for PhD) of unsponsored work after a UK degree — the easiest foothold there is.
  • Global Talent: endorsed leaders in tech, research or arts; no sponsor, fastest settlement.
  • Family/Partner: genuine relationship + £29,000 sponsor income requirement.
  • Innovator Founder: endorsed, innovative business — the entrepreneurship route.

🇺🇸 The main US categories in one line each

  • H-1B: degree-level specialty job + employer sponsor + the March lottery (cap-exempt employers skip it).
  • L-1: intra-company transfer after a year abroad with the firm — no lottery.
  • O-1: extraordinary ability with heavyweight evidence — no cap.
  • F-1 → OPT: study, then 12–36 months of work authorisation — the classic pipeline.
  • EB-2/EB-3: employer-sponsored green cards (EB-2 NIW is self-petitioned for national-interest work).
  • Family & E-2/EB-5: relationship-based and investment-based routes respectively.

Three truths that save applicants months

  1. Target the sponsor, not the visa. In both countries, employers who already sponsor are 10× more likely to sponsor you — the UK publishes its register of licensed sponsors; US H-1B filings are public data.
  2. Timelines drive strategy. H-1B has one lottery a year; UK sponsorship can be done in weeks. Plan backwards from the dates.
  3. Rules move. Salary floors, fees and caps change annually — always confirm on gov.uk or uscis.gov before acting.
FAQ

Common questions

What salary do I need for a UK Skilled Worker visa?

The general threshold is £41,700 or the occupation's 'going rate', whichever is higher — with reductions for Immigration Salary List roles, new entrants and relevant PhDs. The role must also be at RQF level 6 (graduate level) or above.

What are my chances in the H-1B lottery?

Registration selection rates have run roughly 25–35% in recent years. Strategies that avoid the lottery entirely: cap-exempt employers (universities, affiliated nonprofits), L-1 transfers, O-1, or studying first via F-1 → OPT.

Can I apply for these visas without a job offer?

A few routes don't need one: UK Global Talent and Graduate visas, and the US O-1 (needs a petitioner but not a traditional employer) and EB-2 NIW. Everything else in the work category hinges on a sponsoring employer.

How long until permanent residence?

UK: most work and family routes reach Indefinite Leave to Remain after 5 continuous years. US: green-card timing varies enormously — months for spouses of citizens, but years to decades for some employment categories depending on country of birth.

Is this tool immigration advice?

No — it's an educational screener that points you at the categories worth researching. Eligibility depends on your full history and the rules in force on the day you apply; use official sources and, for anything complex, a regulated immigration adviser or attorney.
