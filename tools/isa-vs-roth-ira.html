ISAs and Roth IRAs: the same big idea, different rules

Both wrappers let your money grow completely tax-free — no tax on interest, dividends, gains or withdrawals. The differences are in limits, bonuses and access rules, and picking the right variant can be worth tens of thousands over a lifetime.

🇬🇧 UK ISA family (2026/27 — £20,000 total allowance)

TypeBest forKey rule
Cash ISAShort-term savings, emergency fundInterest tax-free; easy access
Stocks & Shares ISAGoals 5+ years awayAll growth and dividends tax-free

Note: from April 2027 the government plans to cap cash ISA contributions at £12,000/year for under-65s (the overall £20,000 allowance stays).

🇺🇸 Roth vs Traditional IRA (2026)

  • Contribution limit: $7,500 across both ($8,600 if 50+).
  • Roth: pay tax now, withdraw tax-free after 59½. Income phase-out: $153k–$168k single, $242k–$252k joint. No required minimum distributions.
  • Traditional: deduct now (subject to workplace-plan phase-outs), pay income tax on withdrawals.
  • The deciding question: is your tax rate higher now or in retirement? Pay the tax in whichever period is cheaper.

Rules of thumb that usually hold

  • Early-career and lower earners → Roth / Stocks & Shares ISA (your tax rate will likely rise).
  • Peak earners → Traditional / pension contributions (relief at 40%+ now, likely lower rate later).
  • UK first-time buyers under 40 → open a LISA even with £1; the 25% bonus is uncapped free money up to £1,000/yr.
FAQ

Common questions

What is the ISA allowance for 2026/27?

£20,000 per adult across all ISA types combined, with sub-limits of £4,000 for the Lifetime ISA and £9,000 for a Junior ISA. The allowance resets every 6 April and doesn't carry over.

What are the Roth IRA income limits for 2026?

Single filers can contribute fully below $153,000 MAGI, with the allowance phasing out to zero at $168,000. For married filing jointly the range is $242,000–$252,000. Above the limit, the backdoor Roth strategy remains available.

Is the Lifetime ISA worth it?

If you'll buy a first home up to £450,000 or can wait until 60, yes — the 25% bonus is an instant guaranteed return of up to £1,000/year. Withdraw for any other reason and the 25% penalty claws back the bonus plus a little of your own money.

Roth or Traditional — which should I pick?

Compare your marginal tax rate today with your expected rate in retirement. Higher now → Traditional; lower now → Roth; unsure or similar → Roth wins on flexibility (no RMDs, tax-free inheritance, contributions withdrawable anytime).

Can I have both types in the same year?

Yes. UK savers can split £20,000 across several ISA types, and US savers can split the $7,500 IRA limit between Roth and Traditional — the limit is shared, not per-account.
