Side income is taxed on top — at your highest rate

The most common side-hustle shock: because your salary already uses your allowances and lower bands, every pound or dollar of side profit is taxed at your marginal rate from the first unit. A £6,000 Etsy profit on a £35,000 salary is taxed very differently from the same profit with no other income — this calculator stacks them correctly.

🇬🇧 UK rules that matter

  • £1,000 trading allowance: gross side income up to £1,000/year is tax-free and needs no reporting at all.
  • Over £1,000: register for Self Assessment by 5 October after the tax year ends; return and payment due 31 January. You deduct either real expenses or the £1,000 allowance — whichever is bigger (the tool picks for you).
  • Class 4 NICs: 6% on trading profits between £12,570 and £50,270, 2% above.
  • Platform reporting: marketplaces (eBay, Etsy, Vinted, Airbnb, Uber…) now report seller income to HMRC — the "nobody will know" era is over.

🇺🇸 US rules that matter

  • $400 rule: net self-employment profit of $400+ triggers Schedule C/SE filing and 15.3% SE tax (on 92.35% of profit; half deductible).
  • Quarterly estimates: due April 15, June 15, September 15 and January 15 — underpay and the IRS charges interest.
  • 1099-K / 1099-NEC: payment platforms report your income to the IRS; report everything whether or not a form arrives.
  • Deductions: supplies, software, mileage, home-office and platform fees all reduce taxable profit — track them from day one.

The one habit that prevents tax-bill panic

Open a separate savings account and automatically move a fixed slice of every payout — typically 25–30% in the UK, 25–35% in the US — the moment it lands. The calculator above tells you your personal percentage.

FAQ

Common questions

Do I pay tax on a small side hustle in the UK?

Not if gross income is £1,000 or less per tax year — the trading allowance covers it with no reporting. Above £1,000 you must register for Self Assessment, though tax is only due on profit after the allowance or expenses.

When do I have to file Self Assessment?

Register by 5 October following the end of the tax year in which you passed £1,000 of side income; the online return and payment are due by 31 January. Miss it and penalties start at £100.

How much should a US side hustler save for taxes?

A common rule is 25–35% of net profit, covering 15.3% SE tax plus income tax at your bracket. This tool computes your exact figure and splits it into the four quarterly payments.

Can I deduct expenses from side hustle income?

Yes — in the UK, real expenses or the £1,000 trading allowance (whichever is larger); in the US, ordinary and necessary business expenses on Schedule C. Keep receipts and records for at least 5–6 years.

Will HMRC or the IRS know about my platform income?

Assume yes. Digital platforms in both countries now report seller earnings to the tax authorities under information-sharing rules, so unreported income is increasingly easy to detect.
