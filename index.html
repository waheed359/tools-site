By the numbers

Same life decisions. Wildly different rules.

A few of the differences our tools handle automatically — using 2026/27 UK and 2026 US thresholds.

Income Tax

🇬🇧20% / 40% / 45% bands plus a £12,570 personal allowance (tapered over £100k). Scotland runs six separate bands up to 48%.
🇺🇸10% to 37% federal across seven brackets, standard deduction $16,100 (single, 2026) — plus state income tax from 0% to 13.3%.

Retirement Limits

🇬🇧Pension annual allowance £60,000. ISA £20,000. Lifetime ISA £4,000 with a 25% government bonus.
🇺🇸401(k) limit $24,500 ($32,500 with catch-up). IRA $7,500. Roth phases out at $153–168k single income (2026).

Capital Gains

🇬🇧Annual exempt amount £3,000. All assets taxed at 18% / 24% by income band since Oct 2024.
🇺🇸Long-term: 0% / 15% / 20%. Short-term taxed as ordinary income. State CGT adds 0–13.3% on top.

Property Purchase Tax

🇬🇧SDLT bands up to 12%; additional-property surcharge +5%; first-time buyers pay nothing to £300,000.
🇺🇸No federal stamp duty. State transfer taxes run 0% to ~4%, and closing costs add 2–5% of price.

Estate & Inheritance

🇬🇧Nil-rate band £325,000 + residence band £175,000. IHT at 40% above, with the 7-year taper on gifts.
🇺🇸Federal exemption $15M (2026), rate up to 40% above — but ~17 states levy their own taxes from as low as $1M.

Self-Employment

🇬🇧£1,000 trading allowance. Class 4 NICs at 6% / 2%. Self Assessment due 31 January.
🇺🇸1099 + Schedule C. Self-employment tax 15.3%. Quarterly estimates due Apr / Jun / Sep / Jan.

FAQ

Common questions

Are all 25 tools really free and live?

Yes. Every tool on this site is live, fully working and free to use — calculators, generators and planners alike. There's no sign-up, no paywall and no 'coming soon'.

Which tax years do the calculators use?

UK tools use the 2026/27 tax year (6 April 2026 – 5 April 2027). US tools use official IRS figures for the 2025 and 2026 tax years, selectable where relevant. Each tool states its assumptions in the explanation under the results.

What happens to the numbers I enter?

Nothing leaves your device. Every calculation runs in your browser with JavaScript — we don't store inputs or results, and there are no accounts. The optional net worth save uses your own browser's local storage only.

How is the site different for UK and US users?

The 🇬🇧/🇺🇸 toggle in the header switches every tool's logic, not just the labels: HMRC bands, National Insurance, ISAs and SDLT for the UK; IRS brackets, FICA, 401(k)/IRA limits and state taxes for the US.

Can I rely on these results for filing taxes or legal decisions?

The tools use published official thresholds and standard methods, making them excellent for planning and understanding. They aren't a substitute for professional advice — for filings and high-stakes decisions, confirm with a qualified accountant, adviser or lawyer.

Will more tools and countries be added?

Yes — the toolkit grows based on what users ask for. Have a suggestion? Email us via the contact page.
