About AI Money Tools

AI Money Tools exists to answer everyday money questions with actual numbers instead of vague articles. The site offers 25 free, live tools — tax and take-home calculators, mortgage and property analysers, retirement and ISA/401(k) planners, and practical generators for invoices, contracts and wills — built separately for the United Kingdom 🇬🇧 and United States 🇺🇸.

What makes the tools different

  • Region-aware engines. The UK/USA toggle switches the underlying rules, not just labels: HMRC bands, National Insurance, SDLT and student loan plans for the UK; IRS brackets, FICA, 401(k)/IRA limits and state taxes for the US.
  • Current thresholds. UK tools use the 2026/27 tax year; US tools use official IRS figures for 2025 and 2026, sourced from HMRC and IRS publications and reviewed when thresholds change.
  • Transparent methodology. Every result shows its band-by-band working and names the rule that produced it.
  • Privacy by architecture. All calculations run in your browser. We have no accounts, no databases of user inputs, and nothing to sell or leak.

What we are not

We're an educational tool, not a regulated adviser. Results are planning estimates based on published rules — for filings, legal documents and high-stakes decisions, confirm the details with a qualified professional in your jurisdiction. Our full disclaimer explains the boundaries.
