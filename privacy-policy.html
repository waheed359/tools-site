Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This policy explains what information is (and isn't) processed when you use aimoneytools.pro.

1. Calculator inputs — never collected

Every tool on this site runs entirely in your web browser using JavaScript. The figures you type — salaries, balances, names on invoices or wills — are processed on your own device and are never transmitted to, or stored on, our servers. We cannot see them.

2. Local storage

Two convenience features use your browser's own local storage: your UK/US country preference, and the optional "save" in the Net Worth Tracker. This data stays on your device, is never sent to us, and is removed when you clear your browser data or use the tool's Reset button.

3. Information we may process

  • Server logs. Our hosting provider records standard technical logs (IP address, user agent, pages requested) for security and reliability, retained for a limited period.
  • Analytics. If analytics are enabled, we use aggregate, privacy-respecting statistics (pages viewed, approximate country) to understand which tools help people. We do not build individual profiles.
  • Email. If you contact us, we keep the correspondence to answer you. We don't add you to lists.

4. Advertising and cookies

This site is supported by advertising and may display ads served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies (including the DoubleClick cookie) and similar technologies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites, and to measure ad performance.

  • You can opt out of personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info/choices.
  • Details of how Google uses data from partner sites: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.
  • Where required (UK/EEA), a consent banner lets you accept or decline advertising cookies before any are set.

5. Your rights

Depending on your location (UK/EU GDPR, California CCPA and others), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data and to object to processing. Because we deliberately collect almost nothing, most requests are satisfied by clearing your browser data — but email hello@aimoneytools.pro for anything else and we'll help.

6. Children

The site is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

7. Changes

We'll update this page when practices change; the date above reflects the latest revision.
